Prompt Visibility Tracker

The idea

The idea is to compare search visibility through different traveller personas and their questions, instead of relying on one generic brand prompt.

The problem it addresses

A single generic prompt does not represent the range of questions different travellers ask.

Who it helps

SEO strategists and marketers researching how a brand appears in AI-assisted search.

What you can do

Organizes persona-led prompt testing, brand mentions, share of voice, and citation observations across AI platforms.

The intended outcome

Compare brand visibility across traveller personas and AI-search prompts.

Created by SEO Girl - Ami. Review research, generated drafts, and illustrative examples before applying them.
